DELHI NCR — SHARED COMMUTES
What Delhi breathed together — merged, never named.

Government air stations watch only part of Delhi — most streets have no sensor on them at all. Riders who share their trips (the app calls it donating) fill in those gaps: every ride joins everyone else's as totals on this page — never as you. There is no sign-up — no name, no number.

We can't leak what we never take. The proof is three rules below — and the count beside them is live.

01
cut on your phone
the first and last 300 m of a ride are trimmed on-device before it sends anything — home and work never leave the phone
02
merged, never filed
a trip joins totals the moment it lands — no record of any single ride exists at rest, only merged counts
03
3 phones to appear
a street stretch shows up only after 3 different phones cross it inside 3 days — below that it isn't in the data at all
