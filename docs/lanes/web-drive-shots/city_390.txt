02
where the numbers come from

Delhi's government monitors were built to regulate the city, not to tell one commuter what they breathed. 53 live instruments feed this map — the green squares on it — and each live reading counts triple next to the European satellite model (CAMS) and every shared trip.

Where monitors are far apart, the estimate is less sure and the colours go quiet. A district near the edge of coverage says so on its card instead of pretending precision.
