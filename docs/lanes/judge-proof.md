## Part 2 — the weakness list (what a hostile reviewer calls out)

| # | The attack | The mitigation we already have | The in-demo deflection line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Your DPCC ingest **silently falls back** when the site stalls." | Not silent — the 90-min staleness guard drops rows instead of serving stale-as-fresh, and the drop is on the wire three ways: `feeds.dpcc` → `unavailable`, `stations.source` loses `+dpcc`, `count` 54→24. `nearest_sample_km` climbing off 0 at those 30 cells is the earliest tell, published per-point. The WAQI backbone + model prior keep the field live with honestly wider bands. And our own verification lane caught the stall mid-run and wrote it down before any reviewer could. | "Nothing is served as fresher than it is. When the site froze at 2 AM our guard dropped every stale row, /health says the feed's unavailable, and the count falls to 24 — a silent fallback wouldn't have a counter." |
| 2 | "Fringe cells are **>3 km from anything real** — that's extrapolation." | It is — and the API says so. `nearest_sample_km` rides every `/aqi` answer; confidence tiers degrade where coverage <1; past the field bbox it's a typed `outside_service_area` refusal, not an extrapolated lie. The frontier is named in our own report: 36/49 NCR scatter cells >3 km, worst 15.7 km on the north rim — all outside the instrumented ring. | "We publish the distance, not a promise. Inside the instrumented ring the all-station error is 6.2 µg/m³; outside it the API tells you exactly how far the nearest real reading is — which is more honesty than any AQI app gives you about its gaps." |
| 3 | "A **suspect sensor is inside your blend** — IGNOU reads pm25/pm10 ≈ 0.15." | QC guards already run: pm25 > pm10×1.05 is refused, range clamps, staleness drops. Where an instrument reads far off its region the blend lands *between* it and its neighbours — IGNOU's suspect 15.6 served ~29.6 while 3-km neighbours held ~40–46 — a possibly-glitchy low is hedged, not echoed, and the pull dies within ~3 km. The residuals are published unexcluded in the holdout tables; we flagged IGNOU ourselves. | "We don't trust any single instrument either — the blend hedges a sensor that disagrees with its neighbours, and the pull dies within a few kilometres. The reading you're citing is in our published residual table — we flagged it before you did." |
| 4 | "On a calm night, **dumb persistence beats your model**." | True for the pre-blend model — and we published the loss, then fixed it. The live_anchor deploy serves a fitted blend: observation-led inside 2 h, decaying to the model by h+40, convex so it can't do worse than min(model, persistence) pointwise inside the window. Pooled on the night that hurt most: blend 11.2 vs persistence 13.1 vs old model 39.2 — strictly better past h+5. `/v2/skill` grades the deployed blend every hour: 210 pairs tonight. | "It did, once — on one stationary night, and we published that before we fixed it. What's served now is a fitted blend: persistence where persistence is right, the model where it's right, and the scorecard grades the deployed thing in public." |
| 5 | "Your community layer is **22 trips from 14 devices — vaporware**." | The thinness IS the k≥3 gate working at real scale: 42 of 54 tracked corridors are suppressed tonight and the suppression counter is public — `min_k:3`, `active:12/54`, every published row lists its device count (6,4,4,3,4). The store holds aggregates only; there are no per-trip rows at rest to pad density from. | "That's what honest k-anonymity looks like at fourteen users — mostly empty. A seeded map wouldn't have a suppression mechanism at all; we'd rather show you the counter than pad the map." |
