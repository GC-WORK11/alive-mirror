## Install

1. Download the APK from this release's **Assets** and tap it.
2. Android asks once to **install unknown apps** for your browser or
   Files app — allow it.
3. Play Protect may warn "unknown developer" — normal for a sideloaded
   build that isn't Play-listed. Tap **Install anyway**. It's debug-signed;
   recipe: `cd Mobile/android && ./gradlew assembleRelease` at the tag.
4. Open it once. Allow **location** (dose math needs your position) and
   **notifications** (alerts are local — there is no push server).
5. The consent card is the only other decision; sharing off is complete.

## What it does

- **Air map you can tap** — a live PM2.5 field over Delhi-NCR; tap any
  point for the field's answer there, sampled from the cells it paints.
- **Search in your own words** — "bakery ke paas hanuman mandir" parsed
  against a bundled gazetteer that answers before the network does.
- **Route chooser priced in smoke** — every option shows ETA, inhaled
  dose (µg) and what the ride emits, each with its basis stated.
- **Trips** — live dose ticks as you move, reroute when you drift, an
  honestly ranged receipt on arrival.
- **Day and week ledger** — today's dose arc, the week strip, per-trip
  receipts, all reconstructed on-device.
- **Felt log** — one-tap check-in (breath / eyes / energy); after a week
  it reads what heavy-air days do to how you feel — lag-1, not a promise.
- **Offline** — the Delhi map pack downloads once on first open, then
  works air-gapped; the gazetteer is bundled. Last-good numbers read
  "updated N min ago", never stale-as-fresh.
- **Alerts** — local only: stagnation, clean windows, GRAP moves, a
  morning brief. Nothing fires without a real field change.
- **Honestly empty fresh install** — no fake history; it shows today's
  own reads and says so.
- **Your data stays yours** — plain-file stores on-device, carried by
  Android's platform backup; delete-my-data wipes every store.

## Scope — what this build doesn't claim

- **Delhi-NCR only** — outside the box it says so instead of guessing.
- **Live numbers need network** — they follow the ~15-minute station
  cadence; the last field snapshot and map pack are cached.
- **Android only for this tag** — an iOS build exists but is
  unsigned-to-device (simulator only).
- **The phone measures nothing** — dose is modelled (field × breathing
  zone × time), never read off a sensor.
