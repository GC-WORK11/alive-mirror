delhi ncr — shared commutes

Everyone's trips, merged. The map the stations can't make.

Government air stations watch only part of Delhi — most streets have no sensor on them at all. Riders who share their trips (the app calls it donating) fill in those gaps: a ride joins everyone else's as totals on this page — never as you.

We can't leak what we never take. There is no sign-up — no name, no number, no email. A ride's first and last 300 m are cut on your phone before it sends anything, and no record of any single trip is ever written down — only merged counts exist. A street stretch appears below only after 3 different phones have crossed it, so no one commute can be traced back to a person.

Just shared a trip? It's already inside the numbers below — counted, merged, anonymous.
