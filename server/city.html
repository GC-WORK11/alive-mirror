02 where the numbers come from govt monitors + satellite model

Delhi's government monitors were built to regulate the city, not to tell one commuter what they breathed. — live instruments feed this map — the green squares on it — each one outweighing the satellite model's guess three to one, with every shared trip counted too.

Where monitors are far apart, the estimate is less sure and the colours go quiet. A district near the edge of coverage says so on its card instead of pretending precision.
