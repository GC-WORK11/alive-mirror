how it knows

How a breath becomes a number.

01measure

WAQI and DPCC monitors across the NCR report on their own clocks — DPCC posts about every 5 minutes and the map rebuilds every 15 — and every reading lands in the same unit (µg/m³) before anything touches it.

02interpolate

Careful fill-in between stations — kriging — draws the map: a surface, not a guess, and every cell keeps its own error band. We hid 3,550 real station readings and asked the map to guess them — 95.2% landed inside that band. the method ↗

03keep

The air on the route × how hard you're breathing × the minutes you're out, added up along the road on your phone. The engine priced — million road segments under this hour's map to rank routes.
