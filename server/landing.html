How a breath becomes a number.

01measure

WAQI and DPCC instruments across the NCR report on their own cadences — DPCC posts fresh pages about every 5 minutes and the map rebuilds roughly every 15; station readings are converted back to µg/m³ before anything touches them.

02interpolate

Ordinary kriging — careful fill-in between stations — draws the map, a surface, not a guess — and every cell keeps its own error band. 95.2% of 3,550 held-out station-hours landed inside ±2σ. the method ↗

03dose

Concentration × breathing rate × minutes, summed along the road you take — a C++ engine priced — million road segments on it to rank routes; the app runs the same sum over your trips, on-device.
