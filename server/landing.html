the math, in plain words

A trip becomes a dose,
street by street.

01
measure
24 instruments across NCR report a US-EPA sub-index → inverted through EPA breakpoints into real µg/m³, Kalman-smoothed per station.
02
interpolate
stations blend with CAMS model samples, spatial de-bias pulls the model toward instruments, ordinary krige renders 1,600 live cells.
03
price
every road edge carries the smoke on it, this minute — not yesterday's average, never a vibe.
04
route
a C++ A* searches 1.07M priced edges in milliseconds and returns the dose-optimal way, not the fastest.
05
breathe
dose = field × your mode's ventilation × minutes. car AC ≠ windows down ≠ bike — your body knows the difference.
06
aggregate
donated trips anonymize to k≥3 corridors — the city gets a live sensor mesh, nobody gets a file.
the honest band

We quote ± — because we'd rather
say it than fake it.

Every dose carries an uncertainty band calibrated against held-out station hours — krige σ ×3.2, measured 95.2% coverage at ±2σ on 7,240 replayed readings. When coverage is thin, the cell tells you. When a source dies, the board names it.
