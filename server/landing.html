Measure, interpolate, price.

01measure

WAQI and CPCB instruments across NCR report EPA sub-indices — we invert the breakpoint table back to µg/m³ before anything touches them.

02interpolate

Ordinary kriging turns point readings into a continuous field with a real variance per cell. The band is calibrated — 95.2% coverage at ±2σ, measured on 3,550 held-out station-hours. method: σ = √(krige_sd² + (0.12·µ)²) × 3.2 total-error inflation, leave-one-out over CAAQMS station hours vs the OpenAQ archive — field_validation.md

03price

A C++ A* prices every edge by dose — concentration × ventilation × minutes — under legality constraints. The number the app shows is the number the engine minimized.
